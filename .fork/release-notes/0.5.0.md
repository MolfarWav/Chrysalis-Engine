# 🎭 Molfar Vertep 0.5.0

A new look: the Vertep theme, a single top bar and themes of your own. Your apps, chats and settings are kept.

## ✨ What's new

### 🎭 The Vertep look
Molfar Vertep has its own face now: deep black, oxblood red for what is active, cyan for the main button, and the eye-and-rays logo with the **VERTEP** wordmark (ВЕРТЕП in Ukrainian and Russian) in the Kurale typeface.
- **One top bar instead of two.** The logo, the tabs (Molfar and your apps) and the app's own buttons (Split, Fullscreen, Plugins, Rebuild) sit in one row, with Updates and your account on the right. The app gets the height the second row used to take.
- **On a phone** the tabs become one compact switch between Molfar and your apps.
- Vertep is the default for new users. If you had picked Dark or Light before, that choice is kept: pick Vertep in your account menu.

### 🎨 Themes of your own
Your account menu (the user chip, top right) now has a **Theme** list: Vertep, Dark, Light, and any theme you add. Ask Molfar for one ("make me a forest-green theme"): he writes it into your workspace (`themes/<name>.json`), and it appears in the list. A theme recolours the whole interface, Molfar's page included. Molfar has a new built-in skill, `shell-theme`, that tells him how.

### 📖 Roleplay 4.20.0 (through app updates)
Roleplay gets the same look (open **Updates** in the top bar to install it):
- The **Vertep** theme, the default on a fresh install. An existing install keeps its theme; Vertep appears first in Settings → Themes.
- **A navigation rail with names**, from 1280 px wide, in groups: Home and Chats · Characters, Marketplace, Personas, Lorebooks · Presets and Connections · Shortcuts and Tools · Settings at the bottom. Narrower windows keep the icon rail.
- **Sections are full pages** with a large title and a count. Over an open chat they still slide in from the side, so the chat and a reply being written stay where they are.
- **Ukrainian navigation**: Settings → Interface language.

## Other changes
- New route `GET /v1/themes` (shell only, apps cannot read it): the valid workspace themes, plus each refused file with the reason. Theme files accept only hex colours for known colour names.
- Primary buttons use a separate call-to-action colour (`--c-cta`), cyan in Vertep and the accent colour in Dark and Light.
- Kurale (SIL Open Font License) ships with the interface; no font is loaded from the internet.

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.5.0". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.20.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.5.0`.
- **Downloads** below: `Chrysalis-0.5.0-<system>` (file names stay as before so self-update keeps working). The Android APK is debug-signed and cannot update an installed copy.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.5.0` or `:latest`.
