# 🏠 Molfar Vertep 0.6.0

A new Roleplay Home, and your apps can now call Molfar. Your apps, chats and settings are kept.

## ✨ What's new

### 🏠 A new Home in Roleplay 4.21.0 (through app updates)
Open **Updates** in the top bar to install Roleplay 4.21.0. Its Home is built to the Vertep design:
- **Continue story**: your latest chat with its art, the last line, your persona and the lorebook, and **Continue** and **Branches** buttons.
- **Create**: a new character, an imported card (PNG or JSON), or a draft made with Molfar.
- **Ask Molfar**: type what you want or pick a suggestion (a character from a description, your own dashboard, a Litopys review).
- **Achievements** as rings, **recent chats** and **My apps**.
- Home speaks Ukrainian when Settings → Interface language says so, down to "2 год тому".

### 🗂️ Tidy your chats
On Home and on the Chats page, filter chats by **your persona** and by **character** (a group chat counts for each of its members). Delete one chat with its trash button, or press **Select**, tick several (or **Select all**) and delete them together. Every delete asks first, and a chat that is still writing a reply is never deleted. Previews no longer show raw tags like `<sage:tremble>`.

### 🧙 Your apps can call Molfar
"Ask Molfar", "With Molfar" and the **+** in My apps open the Molfar tab on a new chat with the request already typed in. Nothing is sent: you read it, change it if you like, and press send. My apps also switches to your other apps in one click. Only official apps can do this, and only while you are looking at them.

### 🔒 Safer profile moves
Settings → Backup → **Import profile** now checks which version made the backup. A backup from a **newer** Molfar Vertep is refused with "update this one first", so data written by a newer version never lands on an older one (which might not read it). Update the older copy, then import. Backups from this version or older import as before.

## Other changes
- For app authors: `window.chrysalisShell` in an app frame offers `askMolfar(text)`, `apps()` and `openApp(id)` to official apps (one draft every 2 seconds, up to 4000 characters).

## ⬇️ How to get it
- **Launcher users:** run `Molfar-Vertep.bat` and answer **Y** to "Update to release v0.6.0". Then open **Updates** in the top bar for Roleplay 4.21.0.
- **Running from source otherwise:** pull the tag `v0.6.0`.
- **Downloads** below: `Chrysalis-0.6.0-<system>` (file names stay as before so self-update keeps working). The Android APK is debug-signed and cannot update an installed copy.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/molfarwav/molfar-vertep:v0.6.0` or `:latest`.
